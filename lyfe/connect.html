EARLY CONCEPT / FOR ADULTS 18+

Meet through what makes you curious.

Lyfe Connect is for people who would rather begin with a real question, a project, a book, a game, or an oddly specific interest than perform for an algorithm.

A BETTER FIRST MINUTE

Enough context to say something real.

The first version should help two people find a genuine thread quickly. It should not turn either person into a catalogue item or make popularity feel like the point.

01

Choose a few sparks.

Add the questions, interests, projects, and small obsessions you would actually enjoy talking about.

02

See fewer people, more clearly.

A small set of profiles gives each person room for context. No public follower count or popularity score.

03

Open with something specific.

Respond to a real detail. Conversation suggestions can inspire, but they should never speak for you.

LESS PERFORMANCE, MORE SIGNAL

A dating product should not make you feel like content.

Connect is being shaped around slower, more deliberate choices. The product direction avoids endless swiping, public status metrics, and artificial urgency. You should be able to pause, change your mind, and leave without being punished by the interface.

PLANNED PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

Trust is part of the product.

These are requirements for a future build, not claims about features that already exist.

Control stays visible.

Blocking, reporting, pausing, and leaving should be easy to find at the moment they are needed.

Ask before collecting.

Only request information the experience truly needs, explain why, and keep optional choices optional.

Slow down harmful messages.

A review-before-send moment can create space to reconsider language without reading private conversations for engagement.

Make identity clearer.

Verification and scam-resistant design matter because a good conversation begins with confidence that the other person is real.

THE LYFE CONNECTION

Let a good conversation become a shared little plan.

If both people choose it, a conversation seed could become a shared reading list, a date plan, or a small project inside Lyfe. Nothing should move between Connect and Lyfe without clear consent from both sides.
