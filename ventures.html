Aero
Available in Lyfe

Adaptive personal intelligence

Say less without asking Aero to guess more.

Aero works across Today, Tracking, Library, Connect, Gmail and Profile. It builds a permissioned context pack for each turn, keeps episodic, semantic, project and procedural memory separate, and previews changes before they touch Lyfe.

Its v0 promise is measurable: as a successful outcome repeats, the words you need should fall while first-pass intent stays stable. Inferred patterns remain visible candidates until evidence earns persistence. Nothing is silently trained or sent.

Aero is separate from EOS and EOS-F1. EOS remains Sonne Systems research; Aero is the product people use.

EOS-F1
Research ongoing

Evidence-governed learning system

A system that can say “not yet.”

EOS-F1 is testing how a small learning system can retrieve its evidence, refuse unsupported claims, keep uncertain candidates quarantined, and withhold generation when its own fixed checks fail.

The current 0.6B pilot is not released: it passed 7 of 12 boundary probes. A separate dense language baseline improved narrow held-out measures; a sparse-specialist design failed and has been closed. The point is to keep both facts.
