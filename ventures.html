Personal life workspace

Your life, lightly kept.

Lyfe keeps tasks, projects, goals, notes, work, learning, and longer writing in one calm place. You can write to EOS in ordinary language when you want help turning a thought into something useful, while Wander offers a quiet break.

Guest mode stays on this device. Optional sign-in enables private sync. Lyfe installs like an app and works offline.

Networking around useful work

Find the people and rooms your work needs.

Lyfe Connect is a networking platform for collaborators, peers, mentors, projects, and focused communities. Profiles begin with what someone is building, learning, researching, or able to help with, not a follower count.

The private preview brings together a visual work feed, context-rich profiles, small topic Circles, and lightweight project pages. It keeps drafts on the device and sends a next step into Lyfe only after clear approval.
