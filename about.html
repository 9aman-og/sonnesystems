Studio

Small by design. Serious about the work.

Sonne Systems is a small independent research and engineering studio founded by Aman Agarwal. We pick one question at a time, build a fair test, and keep the answer even when it is not the answer we hoped for.

What we are building toward

AI that can adapt without losing its footing.

Some of our work starts with the way brains use short bursts of activity. Some starts with a simpler problem: how should a machine decide whether to reuse what it knows, repair it, wait for more evidence, or learn something new?

The long-term question is easy to say and hard to answer: how can a system keep learning without becoming wasteful, unpredictable, or impossible to test?
