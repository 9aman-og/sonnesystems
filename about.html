Research direction

Learning systems that remain understandable.

The research asks when brain-inspired methods are useful and how a model should respond to new information: reuse what it knows, repair it, wait for more evidence, or learn something new.

The goal is to build systems that can adapt while remaining efficient, predictable, and testable.
