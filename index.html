Research focus

Four hard questions. Clear experiments.

We work on a small set of connected problems where careful controls matter more than impressive language.

Can spiking networks help in practice?

Some neural networks communicate in small bursts instead of updating everything at once. We test when that difference helps under a fair comparison, and when it does not.

When should a model change its structure?

When new information arrives, a system should first ask whether it can reuse, repair, or combine what it already knows before it creates something new.

What can a model guarantee?

We study how much a model can safely ignore, such as a change in lighting, and keep the guarantee tied to the exact situation we tested.

What is the real cost?

We count what a system really spends: work, data movement, calibration, delay, and the hardware underneath it.

When do spiking-convolutional hybrids help?

In one controlled test, a hybrid needed fewer estimated operations on grayscale images. The advantage disappeared on colour images.
